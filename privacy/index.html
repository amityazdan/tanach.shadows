Privacy policy

Shadows Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the צללי התנ״ך (@tanach.shadows) and Bible Shadows (@bible.shadows) YouTube channels, to upload the channels' own animated Bible videos.

It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to those two channels and to read their upload status. It does not collect, store, share or sell any data about viewers or any other person. Google sign-in tokens are kept only on the owner's own computer and are never shared.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
